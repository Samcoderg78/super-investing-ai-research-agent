# Sarvottam Cables Ltd. – Q1 Earnings Brief

### Snapshot
* **Revenue:** ₹1,428 crore (Up 35% year-over-year)
* **Net Profit:** ₹82 crore (Up ~6% year-over-year)
* **Operating Margins:** 11.2% (Contracted by 170 basis points)
* **Stock Reaction:** Shares fell 3.2% to close at ₹1,012 on the NSE following the results.

### Bull Case
* **Strong Demand:** Revenue jumped 35% compared to the year-ago period, driven by robust order growth from power utilities and data-centre developers.
* **Volume Growth:** The underlying volume story remains solid and intact.

### Bear Case
* **Rising Input Costs:** Copper, the company's primary raw material, increased by nearly 14% during the quarter.
* **Margin Compression:** Higher copper prices squeezed operating margins down by 170 basis points to 11.2%, limiting net profit growth to just 6% despite the sharp rise in revenue.

### Open Questions
* Will Sarvottam Cables be able to successfully pass on higher copper costs to its customers to protect its margins?
