## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) is a manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors serving power utilities and data-centre developers [ir_press_release_2026-08-08]. For Q1 FY27, the company reported an order book of ₹3,900 crore, a net profit of ₹82 crore, and maintained full-year revenue growth guidance of 15% to 17% [ir_concall_2026-08-11, ir_press_release_2026-08-08]. 

## Bull case
- **Revenue growth & demand:** Order book increased to ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore a year prior [ir_press_release_2026-08-08]. Demand remains strong from power transmission and data-centre projects, with data-centre cables accounting for approximately 9% of domestic revenue [ir_concall_2026-08-11, ir_press_release_2026-08-08].
- **Capacity expansion:** The new Bharuch (Gujarat) plant, which adds about 30% to cable capacity and is funded through internal accruals and term debt, is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 [ir_concall_2026-08-11, ir_press_release_2026-08-08].
- **Institutional backing:** FII and DII shareholdings stood at 11.8% and 14.6% respectively as of June 2026 [nse_shareholding_2026-07-15].

## Bear case
- **Margin pressure & cost pass-through lag:** Operating and EBITDA margins contracted by 170 basis points year-over-year to 11.2% due to a roughly 14% rise in copper costs [businessdaily_2026-08-09, ir_press_release_2026-08-08]. About two-thirds of contracts feature price-variation clauses, but they reset with a lag of one to two quarters [ir_concall_2026-08-11].
- **Working capital strain:** Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the prior year, primarily driven by slower payments from two large state utility customers [ir_concall_2026-08-11].
- **Regulatory and legal risks:** The company received a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) from the CGST Mumbai office for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [nse_announcement_2026-09-02]. Additionally, historical news reports noted promoter pledging risks [marketwatchindia_2024-03-18], though recent exchange filings show pledged promoter shares declining to 4.1% as of June 2026 [nse_shareholding_2026-07-15].

## Open questions
- **Revenue figure discrepancy:** There is an unresolved discrepancy in reported Q1 FY27 operating revenue. The official company press release states revenue of **₹1,248 crore** (+18% YoY) [ir_press_release_2026-08-08], whereas Business Daily Online reported revenue of **₹1,428 crore** (+35% YoY) [businessdaily_2026-08-09]. 
- **GST demand outcome:** Will the company successfully overturn the ₹46.3 crore GST demand through its planned appeal, or will it materially affect cash flows?
