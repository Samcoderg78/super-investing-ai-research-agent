## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) is a manufacturer of power cables, wires, and conductors experiencing strong demand driven by power utilities and data-centre projects [ir_press_release_2026-08-08]. For Q1 FY27, the company reported an order book of ₹3,900 crore and maintained its full-year revenue growth guidance of 15% to 17% [ir_press_release_2026-08-08, ir_concall_2026-08-11]. However, results show a revenue reporting conflict of ₹1,248 crore versus ₹1,428 crore across sources, alongside margin compression due to rising raw material costs [ir_press_release_2026-08-08, businessdaily_2026-08-09].

## Bull case
- **Robust Order Book and Demand Drivers:** The order book expanded to ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore in the previous year, supported by growth in data-centre and power utility projects [ir_press_release_2026-08-08]. Data-centre cables represent approximately 9% of domestic revenue and are projected by management to double within two years [ir_concall_2026-08-11].
- **Capacity Expansion and Export Growth:** The new manufacturing plant in Bharuch, Gujarat, which increases cable capacity by roughly 30%, is on schedule to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27 [ir_press_release_2026-08-08]. Additionally, export contributions to revenue grew to 21% in Q1 FY27 compared to 16% in Q1 FY26 [ir_press_release_2026-08-08].

## Bear case
- **Raw Material Pressures and Lagged Cost Passthroughs:** Operating and EBITDA margins contracted by 170 basis points year-over-year to 11.2% as copper prices rose nearly 14% during Q1 FY27 [businessdaily_2026-08-09, ir_press_release_2026-08-08]. Management noted that price-variation clauses in contracts reset with a one-to-two-quarter lag [ir_concall_2026-08-11].
- **Regulatory Demands and Working Capital Strain:** The company received a GST demand order of ₹46.3 crore from the Joint Commissioner, CGST, Mumbai, for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [nse_announcement_2026-09-02]. Furthermore, receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the prior year due to slower payments from two large state utility customers [ir_concall_2026-08-11].

## Open questions
- **Revenue Discrepancy:** Why does the official company press release report Q1 FY27 revenue from operations at ₹1,248 crore (an 18% YoY increase), while a news report cites the figure as ₹1,428 crore (a 35% increase) [ir_press_release_2026-08-08, businessdaily_2026-08-09]?
- **Promoter Pledging Discrepancy:** How should investors interpret differing historical and current data regarding promoter pledging, given that an exchange shareholding pattern filing reports pledged promoter holding at 4.1% for June 2026, while an older news report cited pledging at 35% [nse_shareholding_2026-07-15, marketwatchindia_2024-03-18]?
- **GST Demand Impact:** What will be the actual financial and operational outcome of the ₹46.3 crore GST demand order once the planned appeal is processed, given that management expects no material impact [nse_announcement_2026-09-02]?
