## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors [ir_press_release_2026-08-08]. For Q1 FY27, the company reported an order book of ₹3,900 crore [ir_press_release_2026-08-08] and a net profit of ₹82 crore [businessdaily_2026-08-09, ir_press_release_2026-08-08]. Management maintains a full-year FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15 to 17 percent [ir_concall_2026-08-11]. *(Note: An unrelated cable-TV operator mentioned in excluded documents has been excluded from this analysis).*

## Bull case
- **Order Book Expansion:** The company's order book grew to ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up from ₹3,150 crore in the previous year [ir_press_release_2026-08-08], supported by demand from power transmission and data-centre projects [ir_press_release_2026-08-08].
- **Capacity Expansion and Guidance:** The new Bharuch plant in Gujarat is on track to begin commercial production in Q3 FY27, adding approximately 30% to cable capacity [ir_press_release_2026-08-08], while management guides for 15 to 17 percent revenue growth for full-year FY27 [ir_concall_2026-08-11].
- **Export Growth:** International markets continue to scale, with exports contributing 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27 compared to 16% in Q1 FY26 [ir_press_release_2026-08-08].

## Bear case
- **Margin Pressure from Raw Materials:** EBITDA margins contracted by 170 basis points year-over-year to 11.2% in Q1 FY27 due to an approximate 14% rise in copper prices [businessdaily_2026-08-09, ir_press_release_2026-08-08]. Management noted a one- to two-quarter lag in passing these costs through price-variation clauses [ir_concall_2026-08-11].
- **Regulatory and Tax Demands:** The company received an order from the Joint Commissioner, CGST, Mumbai, raising a tax demand of ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) regarding input tax credit claimed for FY2020-21 and FY2021-22 [nse_announcement_2026-09-02].
- **Working Capital Strains:** Receivable days increased from 78 to 96 over the prior year, driven by slower payments from two large state utility customers [ir_concall_2026-08-11].

## Open questions
- **Revenue Discrepancy:** Why does the company's official press release report Q1 FY27 operating revenue at ₹1,248 crore [ir_press_release_2026-08-08], while Business Daily Online reports the figure as ₹1,428 crore [businessdaily_2026-08-09]?
- **Tax Demand Resolution:** What will be the financial and legal outcome of the ₹46.3 crore CGST demand order, and will it require provisioning despite management's confidence [nse_announcement_2026-09-02]?
- **Promoter Pledging Discrepancy:** While the NSE shareholding pattern filing states that promoter pledging stood at 4.1% of promoter holding in June 2026 [nse_shareholding_2026-07-15], older historical reports noted higher historical levels [marketwatchindia_2024-03-18]; how should investors evaluate the evolution and terms of these pledged shares?
- **Working Capital Recovery:** How long will it take to normalize receivable days from the elevated level of 96 days, given that delays stem from state utility customers [ir_concall_2026-08-11]?
